# Gather design
Mode: operate. Host preparing a gathering at home, often on their phone.
A bright party-planning notebook: clean white surfaces, raspberry controls and lilac accents. The task list is the primary workspace, with categories down the side and a compact budget summary. Avoid ornamental imagery; content and small consistent Lucide icons carry the personality.
Palette: paper #faf9fc, ink #292235, muted #70677c, raspberry #b92c65, pale raspberry #fbe9f0, lilac #eee8fa, border #e5e0eb, success #267450.
Type: self-hosted Nunito Sans for approachable readable headings and body; numbers use tabular figures. Left aligned layout, generous group spacing, fine dividers and restrained 12px rounded panels. On mobile navigation wraps above the content; tables become vertically stacked task rows.
Host links use URL fragments. Public invitation pages expose event details only, never budget, checklist or editing keys.
